LEGAL DISCLAIMER (As per Supreme Court of India Guidelines)

As per the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India directions, sale and use of firecrackers is permitted only during specific timings: 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM on Diwali and other festivals. Only Green Crackers approved by PESO/CSIR-NEERI are permitted for sale. We urge all customers to follow local administration guidelines and celebrate responsibly.
