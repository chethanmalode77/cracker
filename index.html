Safety First

Do's & Don'ts

Follow these precautions while using crackers. Safety is our priority!

DO'S

😊
Follow Instructions

Display fireworks as per the instructions mentioned on the pack.

Outdoor Use Only

Use fireworks only outdoor in open spaces.

Use Branded Fireworks

Buy fireworks from authorized/reputed manufacturers only.

Keep Safe Distance

Light only one firework at a time. Others should watch from a safe distance.

Use Agarbatti

Use an agarbatti (incense stick) to ignite the fireworks.

Safe Storage

Store fireworks in a cool and dry place.

Needs Supervision

Always have adult supervision when children are around.

Emergency Water

Keep two buckets of water handy in case of fire or any mishap.

DON'TS

😟
Don't Make Tricks

Never make your own fireworks.

Don't Relight

Never try to re-light or pick up fireworks that have not ignited fully.

Don't Carry It

Never carry fireworks in your pockets.

Do Not Use Glass/Metal

Never shoot fireworks in metal or glass containers.

Do Not Throw

Never point or throw fireworks at another person.

Don't Wear Loose Clothes

Do not wear loose clothing while using fireworks.

Don't Touch It

After the fireworks display never pick up leftover fireworks, they may still be active.

Don't Place Near Candles

Don't store firecrackers near burning candles or lamps.

Don't Drink & Burst

Alcohol causes delayed body responses & crackers might burst early.
